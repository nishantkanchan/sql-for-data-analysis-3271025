# Expert review notes

All translations are `ai_draft`. The translators flagged the verses below as uncertain:
debated plant identities, compressed rules, or source-text problems. Reviewers should
start here, then work through every `importance: "high"` verse.

Corpus caveats that apply everywhere:
- The AH text is the Das & Emmerick GRETIL transcription. Some chapters are shorter than
  printed editions (e.g. AH Su 18, 21, 25, 26 end early or mid-sentence), and some ids hold
  extra "+1" interpolated lines, which translators labelled "[Additional line]".
- Stray variant fragments at the start of ~330 verses were removed from the corpus
  (2026-11-02) *after* some translations were written. Those translations may still carry a
  bracketed "[variant reading …]" note or a first `word_meanings` entry for the fragment,
  which can be trimmed. Affected chapters: AH Su 6, 7, 18-22, 23, 28-30.
