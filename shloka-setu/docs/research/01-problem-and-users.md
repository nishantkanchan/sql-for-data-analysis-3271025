# 01. The problem and the users

Research date: November 2026. Every factual claim carries a source link. Where a number comes from a secondary source (news site, coaching site, social post) it is marked as such. Statements marked **[hypothesis]** are our inferences and need validation through user interviews.

## 1. The problem in one paragraph

BAMS (Ayurvedacharya) students enter college after a Class 12 science stream (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) taught in English or Hindi, with admission through NEET-UG ([MSE 2022 eligibility, reproduced by GJP-IASR](http://ayugjac.edu.in/UG_Course.aspx)). Prior Sanskrit is not an admission requirement ([Pawar 2017, Ayu, PMC5954266](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5954266/)). Yet from the first semester they are expected to read, recite, interpret and write exam answers on Sanskrit verses from the Charaka Samhita and the Ashtanga Hridaya: Samhita Adhyayan is a stand-alone subject in all three professionals, and the Sanskrit paper itself is set "All Sanskrit" ([NCISM AyUG-SA1](https://ncismindia.org/Ay%20UG-SA1.pdf); [RS/SN&AI paper layout, Scribd copy of NCISM 1st prof syllabus](https://www.scribd.com/document/740424936/BAMS-1st-Prof-All-Subject-Syllabus-NCISM)). On top of this, the curriculum is large: 20 courses across 4.5 years of teaching, 6,350 university-exam marks in total (1,700 + 2,100 + 2,550) ([MSE 2022 marks tables](http://ayugjac.edu.in/UG_Course.aspx)), and each course requires both the classical Sanskrit concepts and their modern-science counterparts (for example, Kriya Sharir papers are 60% Ayurveda and 40% modern physiology ([NCISM AyUG-KS](https://ncismindia.org/AyUG-KS.pdf); [text copy](https://pt.slideshare.net/slideshow/ayug-ks-2-pdfgfhfdddfgggffffffrreettrr/287099526))).

## 2. Evidence for the Sanskrit / language barrier

| Evidence | What it shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-sectional study, 200 BAMS students in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai colleges | 61% (122/200) had no Sanskrit at school (p = 0.0025). Students with school Sanskrit scored higher in BAMS university exams (mean 62.91 vs 59.53, p < 0.0001) and on a KAP score (31.06 vs 26.54, p < 0.0001). | Same study | First-year Sanskrit weightage was cut from 250 marks to 100 marks by a CCIM notification in 2012, and the study found that the 1st-year Sanskrit course "makes no difference" to students' knowledge. | [PMC5954266](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5954266/) |
| Regulator response | NCISM issued a revised transitional (induction) curriculum, *Ayurpraveshika*, for first-professional students admitted from 2025-26 (letter dated 03.10.2025). | Medium of instruction | Teaching may be in Sanskrit, Hindi, a regional language or English, and must be English where a college admits students from several states. | Student voice (anecdotal) | Q&A and social posts repeatedly describe 1st-year Sanskrit as "a major drawback" and ask how English-medium students cope with Hindi and Sanskrit. | [Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-tough-is-it-to-deal-with-Sanskrit-in-BAMS-first-year); [Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNA8m9AOcR3/?hl=en) |

Caution: a widely shared LinkedIn post claims "82% of BAMS students struggle with medical Sanskrit" and "89% can't translate basic clinical terms" citing an "Ananta Journal study" ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/lost-translation-how-poor-sanskrit-teaching-failing-vd-dr-divya-nwttc)). We could not locate the underlying study. Do not quote these figures until the primary source is found.

**Evidence gap.** Apart from Pawar 2017 we found little peer-reviewed survey data on Sanskrit difficulty, and none on Hindi-vs-English medium outcomes. A short survey of 100-200 students (Section 7) would give us our own numbers.

**Working estimate of our addressable base [hypothesis built on the figures above]:** about 40,000-43,000 BAMS admissions a year x 4.5 years of coursework gives roughly 180,000-190,000 enrolled BAMS students at any time, plus 1-2 cohorts of interns and repeaters, plus about 30,000-36,000 Ayurveda AIAPGET aspirants a year (overlapping with final-year students and interns). Female students form the majority of AIAPGET Ayurveda candidates (18,357 of 29,824 in 2025, about 62%).

## 4. Exam formats the app must serve

### 4.1 University (professional) examinations, NCISM CBDC 2021-22 onwards
- Three professionals of 18 months each, then a 12-month internship ([MSE 2022, GJP-IASR](http://ayugjac.edu.in/UG_Course.aspx); [NCISM user manual I](https://ncismindia.org/user%20manual%20AyuUG%20I.pdf)).
- Every 100-mark theory paper: 20 MCQ x 1 + 8 SAQ x 5 + 4 LAQ x 10 ([NCISM AyUG-SA1](https://ncismindia.org/Ay%20UG-SA1-1.pdf); [AyUG-KS college copy](https://jjamcollege.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/AyUG-KS.pdf)). So 20% of theory is MCQ and 80% is written answers. Written answers on Samhita are expected to quote shlokas.
- Practical component: practical/clinical, viva, internal assessment and (in some subjects) electives. Pass mark 50% separately in theory and practical ([ayurvedasidhi summary of MSE 2022](https://ayurvedasidhi.com/2025/03/bams-3rd-year-3rd-profession-ncism-syllabus-updated-2025.html); verify against the gazette).
- Grand totals: 1,700 (1st prof), 2,100 (2nd), 2,550 (3rd) ([MSE 2022 tables](http://ayugjac.edu.in/UG_Course.aspx)). Full subject list: `content/data/syllabus.json`.
- Internal assessment: three periodical assessments per term plus term tests ([ayurvedasidhi](https://ayurvedasidhi.com/2025/03/bams-3rd-year-3rd-profession-ncism-syllabus-updated-2025.html)). That means frequent small tests, which suits a daily-quiz habit.

### 4.2 AIAPGET (the PG entrance, the "NEET-PG of AYUSH")
- Conducted by NTA; 120 MCQs, 480 marks, 120 minutes, +4 / -1 ([NTA AIAPGET 2026 information bulletin](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s388a839f2f6f1427879fc33ee4acf4f66/uploads/2026/07/202607111067394464.pdf); [Shiksha](https://www.shiksha.com/medicine-health-sciences/aiapget-exam-pattern)). Syllabus is the whole UG curriculum ([NCISM AIAPGET page](https://ncismindia.org/downloading.php)).
- Competition: AIAPGET 2026 coverage cites about 1 seat per 12 qualified candidates ([Facebook post, weak](https://www.facebook.com/onlyedu.co.in/posts/aiapget-2026-result-declared-27711-qualified-only-2274-seats-1-seat-for-12-candi/1035839432818880/)); AYUSH PG seats are 7,799 against 64,812 UG seats ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2040901)).

### 4.3 National Exit Test (NExT) for AYUSH
- NCISM states NExT applies to AYUSH students admitted from 2021-22 onwards ([NCISM NExT 2026 page](https://ncismindia.org/national-exit-test-2026.php); [NCISM eligibility clarification](https://ncismindia.org/clarification-on-Eligibility-for-next.php)). The first CBDC cohort is exactly our launch audience. The test pattern was not published on the page when we checked, so treat it as a near-term exam to watch.

### 4.4 Government jobs (UPSC and state PSC Ayurveda Medical Officer)
- UPSC ran a Combined Computer Based Recruitment Test for 37 Medical Officer/Research Officer (Ayurveda) posts in 2025 ([Scribd copy of UPSC advertisement](https://www.scribd.com/document/856406999/Andm-Ff-37-Moro-Ayurveda-Ayush-Engl); [CrackTarget syllabus summary](https://cracktarget.com/upsc-ayurvedic-medical-officer-vacancy-2025-syllabus/)).
- States recruit Ayurveda Medical Officers through their PSCs, for example Andhra Pradesh ([APPSC syllabus PDF](https://psc.ap.gov.in/Documents/NotificationDocuments/SYLLABUS%20of%20Ayurveda%20medical%20officer.pdf)) and Uttar Pradesh ([EduRev UPPSC AMO mock series page](https://edurev.in/courses/168785_uppsc-ayurvedic-medical-officer-mock-test-series)). These are MCQ-based and commonly include general studies.

## 5. Study habits and current resources

What we can evidence:
- Students use YouTube lecture channels mapped to the NCISM syllabus (e.g. [Ayurved Studies](https://www.youtube.com/c/AyurvedStudies), [Apta Ayurveda Classes](https://www.youtube.com/@aptayurvedaclasses)), with explicit lists of channels for 1st prof and for Sanskrit ([YouTube: channels for BAMS first year](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yclQZzNUjfM); [Instagram reel naming Ayurveda Marathi, DM Ayu Kingdom, Vivek Palsule](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DLkNrHgRo87/?hl=en)).
- Telegram channels distribute notes and previous-year question papers (e.g. [Ayurveda Siddhi](https://t.me/s/ayurveda_Sidhi?q=%23AIAPGET&before=3854)).
- MCQ apps and test series are popular for AIAPGET: one app claims 15,000+ MCQs and 1,850+ reviews ([AyurPocket](https://ayurpocket.com/)); others offer daily 30-50 question sittings ([CEET](https://www.ceetexams.co.in/daily-mcq-practice), [AyurQuest](https://ayurquest.com/)). See `02-competitors.md`.
- Textbooks are largely in English or Hindi, chosen by the student ([Quora thread](https://www.quora.com/Are-BAMS-books-in-English)); NCISM-aligned Sanskrit books with English translation have been released for the new syllabus ([Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/Ayurvedainformation/posts/new-book-of-sanskrit-released-for-1st-year-bams-according-to-new-syllabus-of-nci/5320206864771597/)).
- Devices: India shipped about 152 million smartphones in 2025 ([IDC summary on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Android/comments/1r6g11e/idc_india_smartphone_market_flat_at_152_million/)); the mass-budget segment (under about US$200) was 40% of shipments in Q3 2025 ([IDC](https://my.idc.com/getdoc.jsp?containerId=prAP53921425)), and the INR 10,000-15,000 band drives the market leader's volume ([Counterpoint](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-share)). Many students will be on mid/low-end Android.

**[hypothesis]** Typical pattern to validate: rote memorisation of shlokas from a Hindi/English guide book a few weeks before exams; YouTube for difficult chapters; Telegram PDFs of PYQs; MCQ apps only in final year for AIAPGET. Little use of the actual Sanskrit text with word-by-word parsing, because no convenient tool exists on the phone.

## 6. Pain points (ranked by how directly Shloka Setu can solve them)

1. **Cannot parse the verse.** Sandhi and samasa hide the words; dictionaries (Monier-Williams, Apte) are English-only and assume grammar knowledge. Evidence: Pawar 2017 above.
2. **Two-language translation load.** Hindi-medium students get English-only resources, English-medium students get Hindi-only commentaries (Hindi tikas dominate classical editions) **[hypothesis, partly supported by the medium-of-instruction rule]**.
3. **Volume.** 20 courses, 517 tagged syllabus topics in our `syllabus.json`, 54 Charaka chapters in SA2 alone ([SA2 syllabus copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/666587504/Ncism-II-Bams-Ayug-sa2)). Students need prioritisation (what is high-yield) and retention (spaced repetition).
4. **Shloka recitation and quoting in answers** are directly marked (15 marks in KS practical, 10 in PV viva). Students need audio and memorisation aids.
5. **Bridging to modern science.** Each subject mixes Ayurvedic and modern content (KS 60:40), and the CBDC asks for "principles and examples in contemporary sciences" in Padartha Vigyan topics ([PV syllabus copy](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/ayug-pv-slybuss-of-bams-first-year-by-ncism-government-ohh-india-1-pdf/273224244)). Students want careful, non-overclaiming correlations.
6. **Exam switching.** The same student moves from university exams (80% written) to AIAPGET/NExT/PSC (100% MCQ). Content needs exam tags.
7. **Cost and connectivity [hypothesis].** Paid coaching is common; budget phones, patchy hostel Wi-Fi.

## 7. User personas

### Persona A: Priya, 1st professional, Hindi medium (primary)
- 18, from a district town in Madhya Pradesh; studied Class 12 in Hindi medium; no Sanskrit after Class 8. Admitted through NEET to a private Ayurveda college.
- Phone: INR 10-12k Android, 4 GB RAM, often on mobile data.
- Goal: pass 1st prof (1,700 marks) without failing Sanskrit or Samhita Adhyayan 1.
- Pain: Ashtanga Hridaya Sutrasthana verses feel like a code; the English textbook explanation adds a second foreign language.
- Needs: Hindi meaning and word-by-word gloss, audio to recite, daily 10-minute quiz in Hindi, "scan this verse from my book".

### Persona B: Arjun, 2nd professional, English medium (primary)
- 20, from Kerala, studying in a college in Karnataka with multi-state students, so teaching is in English.
- Has Charaka Samhita Purvardha (54 chapters) in SA2 plus Dravyaguna's long plant list.
- Pain: Hindi commentaries are the most detailed; English translations are paraphrases that skip word meanings.
- Needs: English word-by-word, IAST transliteration, chapter summaries with exam focus, flashcards for plant rasa-panchaka.

### Persona C: Dr. Sneha, intern / AIAPGET aspirant (secondary, monetisable)
- 23, finished final prof, doing rotatory internship, preparing for AIAPGET (one of about 36,000 Ayurveda candidates).
- Pain: has forgotten 1st and 2nd prof content; needs fast revision of Samhita sutras and frequently asked "shloka completion" MCQs.
- Needs: spaced-repetition decks across all subjects, AIAPGET-style 120-MCQ mocks, verse lookup by fragment.

### Persona D: Vd. Meera, assistant professor (influencer / B2B)
- 32, teaches Samhita and Siddhanta; must deliver activity-based Samhita path sessions required by CBDC (twice the lecture time is non-lecture Samhita activity in SA1, per [NCISM AyUG-SA1](https://ncismindia.org/Ay%20UG-SA1.pdf)).
- Needs: reliable verse text with references, shareable quizzes for a class, ability to flag AI errors (feeds our expert-review workflow).

## 8. Implications for the product

- Default to **Hindi + English side by side**, with IAST and Devanagari; let the user pick the primary language.
- Tag every verse, card and quiz item with `syllabus.json` topic ids so the app can say "this is in your 2nd prof SA2, Charaka Sutrasthana 26".
- Ship **SA1 (AH Su 1-15, CS Su 1-12) first**: it is the first Samhita every student meets, and we already hold the AH corpus.
- Make MCQ practice follow the NCISM ratio (MCQ is only 20% of university theory) and also offer AIAPGET/NExT modes.
- Offline-first for budget phones (see `04-tech-landscape.md`).
- Validate the hypotheses above with a 10-question survey and 8-10 interviews across one Hindi-belt and one southern college before building beyond SA1.
