## 2. Product-by-product notes

### 2.1 AyurPocket (closest all-round competitor)
- **What it does:** Samhitas library (Charaka, Sushruta, Ashtanga Hridaya), year-wise BAMS notes for all professionals, daily quiz with streaks (60 s per MCQ), AIAPGET/govt exam PYQs and mocks with all-India rank, Dravyaguna plant list, "Ayur Mind Lab" games (match the column, number recall, one-liner flashcards), many utilities (progress/syllabus tracker, planner, attendance, image-to-PDF), news. Sources: [ayurpocket.com](https://ayurpocket.com/), [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ayurveda.pocket&hl=en_IN).
- **Scale:** 10K+ downloads, updated 24 Oct 2026 ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ayurveda.pocket&hl=en_IN)); the website claims 15,000+ MCQs and "4.9, 1,850+ reviews" ([site](https://ayurpocket.com/), self-reported).
- **Pricing:** free core; "some advanced content and eBooks are part of AyurPocket Premium or can be unlocked with coins" ([site](https://ayurpocket.com/)). INR prices not published.
- **Gaps:** shlokas come with Hindi translation but no word-by-word parsing is advertised; no camera/OCR lookup; no spaced-repetition scheduling (games and one-liners instead); no AI tutor; breadth over depth on the Sanskrit itself.

### 2.2 AyurQuest (AIAPGET question bank)
- **What it does:** random/samhita/subject/sthana/chapter-wise quizzes, test series, mock tests, AIAPGET PYQs, "55,500+ questions with references", scorecards with negative marking, daily tests, chapter-wise lectures of Samhitas ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ayurquest-aiapget-ayurveda/id6499452927); [ayurquest.com](https://ayurquest.com/), "50 questions a day", AIAPGET and UPSC Ayurveda).
- **Rating:** 4.5 from 219 ratings on the Indian App Store; free; wallet points for referrals and question reports ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ayurquest-aiapget-ayurveda/id6499452927)).
- **Gaps:** exam drill only; no verse reading, translation or Hindi support advertised; aimed at final-year/interns, not at the 1st-prof language problem.

### 2.3 CEET Ayurveda for AIAPGET
- **What it does:** MCQ bank, mock exams, daily quiz; website runs 30 questions a day in 20-minute sittings across 60+ subjects for AIAPGET, PSC, UPSC, NTET ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.co.ceetexams.ceet&hl=en_IN); [ceetexams.co.in](https://www.ceetexams.co.in/daily-mcq-practice)).
- **Rating:** 5.0 from 15 reviews, 10K+ downloads, updated 22 Oct 2026; listing says no ads ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.co.ceetexams.ceet&hl=en_IN)).
- **Gaps:** same as AyurQuest.

### 2.4 Pratyaksh Ayurveda (coaching platform)
- **What it does:** live and recorded classes for BAMS I-III, AIAPGET, AMO and clinical orientation; mock tests, MCQ library with references, analytics, notes, doubt sessions ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ayurveda.pratyaksh&hl=en_IN); [pratyakshayurveda.in](https://pratyakshayurveda.in/)).

### 2.6 Charak Samhita Online (Charak Samhita New Edition)
- **What it does:** a MediaWiki-based contemporary edition of the whole Charaka Samhita with Sanskrit text, English translation, commentary and glossary, by the Charak Samhita Research, Training and Skill Development Centre (CSRTSDC), Jamnagar ([carakasamhitaonline.com](https://www.carakasamhitaonline.com/); [project page](https://www.carakasamhitaonline.com/index.php/Charak_Samhita_New_Edition_(Wiki)_Project); [Basisht, PMC11782805](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11782805/)).
- **Pricing/licence:** free; footer says content is under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless otherwise noted, and the project calls itself "pure non-commercial" ([home page](https://www.carakasamhitaonline.com/); [project page](https://www.carakasamhitaonline.com/index.php/Charak_Samhita_New_Edition_(Wiki)_Project)). - **Gaps:** unstructured PDFs, uncertain copyright, no interactivity.

## 3. Gaps across the market

1. **Language bridge is missing.** Readers show Sanskrit plus a translation; MCQ apps skip the Sanskrit. Nobody shows *each word* with Hindi and English meaning and grammar, which is what a student without school Sanskrit needs ([Pawar 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5954266/)).
2. **No camera path from the textbook.** Students study from printed books; no app lets them photograph a verse and get it identified, parsed and explained.
3. **No proper spaced repetition.** Streaks, daily quizzes and games exist (AyurPocket, CEET), but not SRS scheduling of shlokas and concepts.
4. **Weak syllabus mapping for the classical texts.** Apps map notes to years, but not each verse to the NCISM topic it serves (our `syllabus.json` gives 517 tagged topics with chapter refs).
5. **Trust and provenance.** Most apps do not state where their Sanskrit text came from or who reviewed translations. Expert review status shown per item is a differentiator.
6. **Hindi-first UX is rare** outside AyurPocket and the Vaidik Apps reader.

## 4. Positioning statement

**For** BAMS students in India, especially first- and second-professional students who did not study Sanskrit at school, **who** must read, recite and write answers on Charaka and Ashtanga Hridaya verses in exams, **Shloka Setu is** a phone-first Sanskrit-to-understanding bridge **that** turns any shloka (tapped in the app or photographed from a textbook) into word-by-word Hindi and English meanings, a plain explanation, a careful modern-science link, and spaced-repetition cards and quizzes tagged to the NCISM syllabus. **Unlike** MCQ banks (AyurQuest, CEET), all-in-one note apps (AyurPocket) or text readers (Charak Samhita apps, NIIMH e-Samhita, Charak Samhita Online), **Shloka Setu** teaches the student to *understand the verse itself*, in their own language, with every AI-generated explanation labelled and progressively expert-reviewed.

Wedge: Samhita Adhyayan 1 (AH Sutrasthana 1-15 + CS Sutrasthana 1-12) for 1st-prof students, where the language pain is sharpest and no competitor goes deep. Expand to SA2/SA3 and then cross-subject AIAPGET/NExT revision, where MCQ competitors are strong but have no verse-level understanding layer.
